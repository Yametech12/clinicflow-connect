ClinicFlow PH
AI front desk for Philippine dental clinics
Connect once, reply forever

Your clinic Messenger, answered in seconds. Your prices, your accounts.

ClinicFlow PH sits on your clinic Facebook Page. When a patient asks how much a cleaning costs, it answers with the price from your own price sheet, in warm Taglish, in at most three short sentences. When the patient is ready, it collects their name, mobile number, and preferred schedule, then writes the booking into your own Google Sheet, your own Google Calendar, and pings your Telegram.

1. Patients ask on Messenger

Prices, hours, services. The bot only quotes what is written in your price sheet. Change a price in the sheet and the next answer uses it.

2. The bot collects the booking

Name, mobile number, preferred date and time. Nothing is booked until all three are given.

3. You see it instantly

A row in your booking sheet, an event on your calendar, and a Telegram alert on your phone.
