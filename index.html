How it works

From a patient's first message to a booking on your phone, in three steps.

1

Patients ask on Messenger

Prices, hours, services. The bot only quotes what is written in your price sheet. Change a price in the sheet and the next answer uses it.

2

The bot collects the booking

Name, mobile number, preferred date and time. Nothing is booked until all three are given.

3

You see it instantly

A row in your booking sheet, an event on your calendar, and a Telegram alert on your phone.
