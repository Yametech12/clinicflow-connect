Connect once, reply forever

Reply to every patient in seconds. Never lose a booking again.

ClinicFlow answers your clinic's Messenger for you. It quotes the prices from your own price sheet, asks for the patient's name, mobile number, and preferred schedule, then writes the booking into your own Google Sheet and Calendar.

Every confirmed booking also reaches your phone by Telegram. Kahit sarado na ang clinic, may sumasagot sa pasyente.
