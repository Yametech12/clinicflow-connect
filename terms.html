Terms

The terms of a ClinicFlow pilot setup.

What the pilot setup is

We set up a Messenger bot on your own Page: it answers price and service questions from your price sheet, collects each patient's name, mobile number, and preferred schedule, writes the booking into your own Google Sheet and Calendar, and sends you a Telegram alert for every confirmed booking. Setup takes 3 days, done by us on your own accounts.

What we need from you

Send us your price list and clinic hours, then sign in to your own Facebook, Google, and Telegram using the steps in your dashboard. About 15 minutes of your time, total. At the end we walk you through the bot live on your own Page.

Price and payment

Pilot setup is ₱5,500 one time. Regular setup is ₱12,000. Pilot terms: 50 percent deposit (₱2,750) by GCash, Maya, or InstaPay to start, and the balance only at the live walkthrough, when you see the bot answering on your own Page. Connecting your accounts is free and does not start any payment, and we confirm the pilot with you first before any payment is due.

Your accounts and files

You own every account and file; we only install and configure. Every account stays under your name, and you sign in on each provider's own page. ClinicFlow never sees your passwords.

Ending the service

You can disconnect ClinicFlow anytime from your Facebook Settings and your Google Account permissions, and you can block the Telegram alert bot anytime to stop alerts.

Questions

Questions about these terms? Email us at epimetheus.support@gmail.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
